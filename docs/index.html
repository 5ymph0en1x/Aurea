The cubic BD-rate is cross-checked with piecewise-linear integration. JPEG points use libjpeg-turbo and retain the smaller optimized sequential/progressive output. AUR2 v28 hands kryte's surplus luminance back to its chroma on the diffuse-dark field (eleven of twelve originals stay byte-identical). AUR2 v29 adds a quality-keyed perceptual texture law, calibrated on this sheet's own rate-matched protocol: at low quality the trellis keeps texture instead of MSE (lambda 8, activity-modulated, C2 weight 1.75) and the decoder restores punch (contrast-floored XSharpen + a cored unsharp mask); at high quality the anti-ring sigma filter switches off. AUR2 v30 adds the EOB-driven luma step field above q30: the transmitted end-of-block positions carry the intra-block activity (rank correlation 0.887 with squared error), and both sides derive a zero-bit log-neutral step refinement toward the blocks that hold the error. Luminance BD-rate against optimized JPEG 4:2:0 turns negative on all twelve images; v31 extends the same field to the two chroma channels, each driven by its own transmitted EOBs; v32 mutes the sharpening chain and softens the luma field on the diffuse-dark signature, closing the last SSIM cell. Every cubic BD-rate cell of this report, on every image, every metric and both JPEG profiles, is now negative.

Protocol and reproducibility

The visual sheet uses two rate-matched operating points per image: AUREA q20 against the nearest-size JPEG 4:2:0, then AUREA q75 against the nearest-size JPEG 4:4:4. Across the 24 pairs, the mean absolute size mismatch is 2.19% and AUREA wins RGB PSNR 24/24, by +1.96 dB on average.

The analytical frontier is broader: AUREA q20/40/60/80 against optimized JPEG q5–100, with RGB/Y/Cb/Cr PSNR, SSIM-Y and CIEDE2000 measured at native resolution. LPIPS (VGG) and DISTS are evaluated on a 1024 px perceptual grid for the visual sheet.
